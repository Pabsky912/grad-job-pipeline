# Quick prompts

Short things to say to your AI once you're set up. It will find the right
method in `docs/` by itself; these just show what's possible.

**Getting started**
- "Set me up." (runs onboarding)
- "Here's my CV: [attach]. Build my fact bank and tell me what's missing."
- "Show me 10 real postings across my tracks so I can tell you what I like."

**Finding things**
- "Anything new today?" (quick Scout)
- "Run Scout." (full search, judged, added to my tracker)
- "Is this one for me? [link]"
- "Here are the jobs from my LinkedIn alert: [paste]. Judge them."
- "Find Master's programmes in [field] in [countries] starting 2027, with funding."

**Applying**
- "Tailor my CV for [tracker id or link]."
- "Draft a cover letter for the Acme role, 350 words."
- "Help me answer this form question in 200 words: [question]."

**People**
- "Write a cold email to [professor] at [university] asking about RA roles."
- "Who should I contact on LinkedIn this week?"
- "They accepted! Draft the follow-up."

**Keeping track**
- "I applied to Acme today." / "I got an interview at Beta!" / "Not for me: too much sales."
- "What's due in the next two weeks?"
- "Build my dashboard and calendar."
- "Set up a routine every 3 days."

**Changing your mind**
- "I don't want consulting any more."
- "Add Copenhagen to my places."
- "I'm now open to starting in March."
